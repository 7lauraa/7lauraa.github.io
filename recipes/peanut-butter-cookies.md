# peanut butter cookies

```
amount		ingredient		notes:
1 each		eggs
1 c.		peanut butter		prefer smooth, crunchy can work
1 c.		white sugar

METHOD:

in a large bowl, add the WHITE SUGAR, EGGS, and PEANUT BUTTER and mix until thoroughly combined.

take medium sized chunks out of the mix and roll into ball in your hands.

add balls to a lined baking tray, and press each ball into a flat disk with the back of a fork.

BAKING:

PREHEAT oven to 180C

BAKE for 15 minutes.
```
