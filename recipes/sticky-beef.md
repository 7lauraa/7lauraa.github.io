# sticky beef
```
*DROP 1:*
amount		ingredient		notes:
1 lb		ground beef
3 cloves	minced garlic
1/2 can.	beer

*DROP 2:*
amount		ingredient		notes:
1/2 c.		brown sugar
1/2 c.		soy sauce
2 tbsp.		sesame oil
1 tsp.		fresh ginger
1 tsp.		crushed red pepper

*DROP 3:*
amount		ingredient		notes:
3 each		sliced spring onion
1 tbsp.		sesame seeds
to serve.	basmati rice

METHOD:

DROP 1
in a large frying pan, fry the GROUND BEEF and MINCED GARLIC until the beef is cooked. slowly add the beer once the beef starts to dry.

DROP 2
in a large bowl, whisk the BROWN SUGAR, SOY SAUCE, SESAME OIL, FRESH GINGER and CRUSHED RED PEPPER until thoroughly mixed. add DROP 2 to DROP 1 when beef is cooked. fry for 2 minutes.

DROP 3
cook the BASMATI RICE in a rice cooker, then add DROP 3 to DROP 1+2 and fry for 2 more minutes.
```
